# Customization

These pages cover Snap Templates features beyond the core [Config](https://github.com/athoscommerce/snap/blob/main/docs/TEMPLATES_CONFIG.md) and [Theming](https://github.com/athoscommerce/snap/blob/main/docs/TEMPLATES_THEMING.md). Using a standard Snap integration? See [Snap Customization](https://github.com/athoscommerce/snap/blob/main/docs/SNAP_CUSTOMIZATION.md) instead.

- **[Variants](https://github.com/athoscommerce/snap/blob/main/docs/TEMPLATES_VARIANTS.md)** — how variant selections render on results, variant settings, and realtime variants.
- **[Quickview](https://github.com/athoscommerce/snap/blob/main/docs/TEMPLATES_QUICKVIEW.md)** — preview a product in a modal or slide-out panel without leaving the results.
- **[Badges](https://github.com/athoscommerce/snap/blob/main/docs/TEMPLATES_BADGES.md)** — how badges render on results, and registering custom badge components.
- **[Background Filters](https://github.com/athoscommerce/snap/blob/main/docs/TEMPLATES_BACKGROUND_FILTERS.md)** — category pages and custom background filters through the platform and common plugins.
- **[Tabbed Search](https://github.com/athoscommerce/snap/blob/main/docs/REFERENCE_TABBED_SEARCH.md)** — span search and autocomplete across multiple catalogs, with a tab for each.
- **[Config Validation & Linting](https://github.com/athoscommerce/snap/blob/main/docs/REFERENCE_CONFIG_VALIDATION.md)** — how the compiler and ESLint rules check your config, and what their errors mean.
